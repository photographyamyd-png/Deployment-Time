01
No Subcontracting. Ever.

Every service GLC quotes is performed by our own operators on our own equipment. We don't broker work. If we're on your project, we're doing the work — not someone you've never met.

02
Owner-Accessible. Always.

Project managers call us and talk to a decision-maker. Questions get answered. Scope changes get discussed. There's no call centre, no ticketing system, no waiting.

03
Line-Item Transparency.

Our quotes are detailed, itemized, and locked before we mobilize. No scope creep surprises — we define scope precisely upfront, then hold to it. Certificate of insurance on request.

04
Built for Commercial.

We're not a residential landscaper who occasionally takes on commercial jobs. Commercial projects are our primary work — our equipment, crews, and processes are built around that.

How We Work

From first call to final grade.

A repeatable process that keeps your project moving — and keeps you informed at every step without chasing us.

Request a Site Assessment
01
Discovery Call

You speak directly with the owner. We discuss your project scope, timeline, site conditions, and access constraints. No forms, no call centres — straight answer, first call.

02
Site Visit & Assessment

We visit the site at no charge. We assess soil conditions, access, grade requirements, and underground utility proximity before quoting anything. No surprises later.

03
Line-Item Quote

You receive a detailed, itemized written quote with locked scope. What we quote is what we deliver — no scope creep, no surprise addendums mid-project.

04
Mobilization & Execution

Typical mobilization within 5–10 business days of contract execution. Our crews arrive with the right machines for your site and complete work to grade spec, on time.
