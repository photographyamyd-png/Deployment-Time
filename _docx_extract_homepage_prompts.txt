H2: "Frequently Asked Questions — Ground Level Contracting"

Q1: What services does Ground Level Contracting offer?
A1: We provide four core service areas: excavation and site preparation, foundations and civil infrastructure, drainage and hardscaping, and commercial snow removal. Within those areas, we handle everything from residential foundation digs and custom pool excavation to subdivision site servicing, mass earthworks, armour stone retaining walls, interlock patios, drain tile installation, and 24/7 commercial snow removal with SLA contracts. If it involves the ground in Barrie or Simcoe County, we do it.

Q2: What areas do you serve?
A2: We serve Barrie, Orillia, Innisfil, Wasaga Beach, Angus, Springwater, Oro-Medonte, New Tecumseth, Collingwood, Midland, Bradford, and throughout Simcoe County and Central Ontario. We're based in Simcoe County — we're not a company dispatching from far away.

Q3: Do you work on both residential and commercial projects?
A3: Yes. We serve homeowners, custom home builders, residential developers, general contractors, commercial property owners, property managers, and municipalities. The right equipment, proper insurance, and professional project management are standard on every project — regardless of size.

Q4: Are you licensed and insured?
A4: Yes. Ground Level Contracting carries full commercial general liability insurance, WSIB registration in good standing, and completes all work to Ontario Building Code standards. Certificates of insurance are provided as standard on commercial projects. We also carry commercial vehicle insurance and hold all required contractor licences for our scope of work in Ontario.

Q5: How do I get a quote?
A5: The best way to get an accurate quote is a free on-site assessment. We visit your property, assess the scope, and provide a detailed written quote — no vague estimates, no surprise charges. Call us directly, use the contact form on this site, or send us a message and we'll book a site visit within 24–48 hours.

Q6: Do you offer free estimates?
A6: Yes. All estimates start with a free on-site visit. We don't quote major civil, excavation, or hardscaping projects over the phone because the site conditions determine the actual scope. For commercial snow removal, we conduct a free property assessment and provide a written quote with SLA terms before any contract is signed.
